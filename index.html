Pahunch · Arriving

Station se shaadi tak

From the train to the wedding in two easy steps.

Utariye New Cooch Behar par

Get off at New Cooch Behar (NCB). Nearly every long-distance train stops here, about 5 km outside town. The small Cooch Behar (COB) station in town doesn't get these trains, so don't wait for it.

Jaankari · Good to know

Kaam ki baatein

टिकट कब खुलेगा

Book on the right morning

Train bookings open 60 days before the journey, at 8 AM. For travel on 26 January 2027 that's 27 November 2026. Each train above shows its own opening date.

कोहरे का ध्यान

Leave room for fog

Late-January fog often holds up trains from Delhi and Rajasthan by a few hours. Aim to arrive on the 27th so a delay doesn't cost you a function.

ठंड रहेगी

Pack a shawl

Days are mild, but nights drop to around 10°C. Bring a shawl or light jacket for the evening functions.

फ़ुर्सत मिले तो

A morning in town

Cooch Behar Rajbari (the old palace), the Madan Mohan temple and Sagardighi are all in town and make an easy morning out.
